# Support

*TennisBuddy Score Tracker.* [Nederlands](nl/support)

A question, a problem or an idea: write to
[tennisbuddy@achthoven.nl](mailto:tennisbuddy@achthoven.nl). Say which watch and phone you use,
and what happened.

## How it works

- **Scoring.** Start a match on the watch: singles or doubles, and who serves first. Touch the
  top half of the screen for a point to the opponents, the bottom half for a point to your
  side. Undo takes back the last point.
- **Doubles.** Touch the marker of the serving side to choose who serves; the watch keeps the
  order from there.
- **The history.** A finished match appears on the phone by itself when the watch and the phone
  are paired and both apps are installed. Open the app on the phone to see it.
- **Names.** Prepare a match on the phone with the names of the players, and the watch offers
  it the next time you start a match.

## A match does not appear on the phone

- Check that the watch is paired with the phone and that TennisBuddy is installed on both.
- Open TennisBuddy on the phone; it then also fetches what it missed.
- A match that was ended with "End match" is not kept; only a finished match is.

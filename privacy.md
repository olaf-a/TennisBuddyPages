# Privacy policy

*TennisBuddy Score Tracker, for iPhone, Apple Watch, Android phones and Wear OS watches.
Last changed on 15 October 2026.* [Nederlands](nl/privacy)

TennisBuddy collects nothing about you and shares nothing with anyone.

## What the apps keep, and where

- **Your matches**: the points as they were played, when they were played, and the names you
  give the players when you prepare a match. They are kept on your watch while a match is
  played, and on your phone afterwards. Removing the app removes them.
- Nothing else. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the
  apps do not contact any server of the maker.

## How a match gets from the watch to the phone

The watch and the phone pass a match to each other through the connection the system provides
for that: Watch Connectivity on Apple devices, and the Wearable Data Layer of Google Play
services on Android. On Android, Google Play services may carry it through Google's servers
when the watch and the phone are not near each other; that is Google's service, under
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The maker of TennisBuddy
receives none of it.

## Permissions

- **Health, on Apple Watch.** While a match is played, the app holds a workout session so that
  it stays on screen when you raise your wrist. That needs your permission for Health. The app
  reads nothing from Health and saves nothing to it. Refusing changes only that the watch goes
  back to the watch face.
- **Notifications, on Wear OS.** A match in progress is shown on the watch face as an ongoing
  activity, which is a notification.
- **Vibration, on Wear OS.** A point and a change of ends are confirmed with a vibration.

## Children

The apps are not directed at children, and they collect nothing from anyone.
